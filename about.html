About Trigu Italia

A family rooted in Sardinia, bringing its finest traditions to your table.

Our Story

Born from a love of Sardinia

Trigu Italia is a family business, owned and run by Jon Brownstein and his Sardinian sons, Gabriele and Lorenzo. We have a personal stake in preserving Sardinian heritage through sustainable farming practices and creating jobs on the island.

We work directly with small-scale producers across Sardinia — shepherds, cheesemakers, fishermen, and farmers — to bring their extraordinary products to tables in the United States. Every item in our catalog represents a tradition worth preserving.

Our products travel from farm to your door in under three days, ensuring the same freshness you'd experience sitting at a table in Sardinia itself.
