Artisan Tours 2026

A week-long full immersion into the artisan traditions of Sardinia.

Our chef tours take small groups deep into the heart of Sardinian food culture — meeting shepherds, cheesemakers, winemakers, and fishermen who carry on centuries-old traditions. These are not tourist experiences. They are professional and personal relationships built over decades.
